## Full description (EN)

Garir Khata (গাড়ির খাতা) helps motorcycle and car owners track:

- Fuel fill-ups and mileage (km/L)
- Expenses by category
- Services, engine oil, repairs, tyres & battery
- Documents (fitness, tax token, insurance) with reminders
- Reports and history timeline
- Local encrypted backups (.gkbackup)

Your data stays on your phone. Bangla and English supported.
